    How Blue Makes A Person Feel

  • Blue ressembles sadness, strength, calmness, mental health & productivity

  • The colour blue has a lot of emotion. A light blue is seen as calm and serene. A cool colour blue can feel like a cold, distant, frosty colour. The colour blue also represents the male gender.

    • How Purple Makes A Person Feel

  • Purple represents royalty, mystery, imagination, wealth, rarity & wisdom.

  • The colour purple is mixed with culture and price. Purple is strong costly royalty colour.

    • How Pink Makes A Person Feel

  • Pink ressembles kindness, warmth, innocence, femininity, calmness, & romance.

  • The colour pink has a peaceful meaning.

    • How Black Makes A Person Feel

  • Black symbolizes power, elegance, mystery, fear

  • Black is associated with greif because people wear black to a funeral. It also involves boldness, elegance, intelligence, & authority. Black is not a real colour.

    • How White Makes A Person Feel

  • White links with cleanliness, purity, innocence, peacefulness.

  • White is a dull empty colour. It's mixed heavily with religion and peacefulness.
