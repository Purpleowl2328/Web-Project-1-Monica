Why I Feel Colour Psychology is Important/Interesting

    Colour psychology is important because it's how some people express themselves. I see colour psychology like the movie Inside Out. In inside out each character has their own colour. For example, Anger is the colour red and fear is purple. Colours have meaning.

    How Red Makes a Person Feel

  • Red ressembles love, excitement, passion, rage, and power. Red is the second most noticable colour (As yellow is first). It stimulates high blood pressure, Increases heart rate, boosts metabolism, and more!

    • How Orange Makes a Person Feel

  • Orange is a bright, exciting colour. It usually understood as the "halloween colour". Many people find orange a very overwhelming and intense colour.

  • Orange ressembles energy, spirit, happiness, pride, warmth, & attention.

    • (Orange also ressembles autumnal vibes).

    How Yellow Makes a Person Feel

  • Yellow is a warm, very bright, and intense colour. It can grab lots of attention because it's usually used with important information. For example, traffic and danger signs, ads, etc. The colour yellow causes eye strain and can be hard to read.

  • The colour yellow ressembles Strength, Independence, Joy, Light, Dignity.

    • How Green Makes a Person feel

  • The colour green ressembles Envy/jealousy, Luck, Nature, Health & Peace.

  • The colour green is a nature seen colour that can make people feel calm and relaxed. Being surrounded or seeing green can raise heath and lower stress.
  • Green also has evil too. The colour green can represent envy and disgust.
